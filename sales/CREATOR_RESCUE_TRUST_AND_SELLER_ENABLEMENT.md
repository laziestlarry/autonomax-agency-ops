# Creator Rescue — Trust, Marketing & Seller Enablement

## Purpose
Enable Creator Rescue sellers to earn trust before asking for money and move qualified creators through Scan → Proof → Pilot → Delivery → Momentum without unsupported promises.

## Positioning
Creator Rescue is not generic AI video production and not a guaranteed-growth service. It is a channel-specific diagnosis and production experiment for creators who already have audience equity but whose content engine appears stalled, inconsistent, or underperforming.

Core promise: **We study what your public channel evidence says, identify a high-leverage next content experiment, build the experiment, and give you a measurable production package.**

## Trust Stack
1. **Specificity before claims** — show observations from the prospect's actual channel.
2. **Evidence before pitch** — separate observed facts, hypotheses, and unknown private analytics.
3. **Proof before purchase** — provide one useful concept/packaging artifact free.
4. **Scope before price** — buyer knows exactly what Pilot contains and does not contain.
5. **No outcome guarantees** — sell research, production and experimentation; never promise views/subscribers/revenue.
6. **Traceable fulfillment** — order → production → QA → delivery → acceptance.
7. **Human control** — creator approves brand-sensitive direction; no autonomous publishing without permission.
8. **Rights discipline** — use owned/licensed/permitted assets and flag uncertain rights before delivery.

### How it works
**1. We inspect** — public videos, topics, cadence, packaging patterns and observable performance signals.

**2. We diagnose** — we distinguish evidence from hypotheses and identify the strongest next experiment.

**3. We prove** — before a purchase, we show a useful channel-specific idea: concept, hook, title or thumbnail direction.

**4. You decide** — if the diagnosis is useful, purchase a Creator Rescue Pilot. No subscription is required to test us.

**5. We build** — research, script/storyboard, packaging variants, production assets, Shorts derivatives and publishing package according to agreed scope.

**6. We learn** — where the creator shares permitted performance data, the next cycle uses the evidence rather than guesswork.

## Seller Qualification Card
Do not sell before qualifying.

PASS when most are true:
- creator has an established body of content/audience equity
- observable stagnation, inconsistency or packaging/content opportunity exists
- niche has commercial value or creator demonstrates purchasing capacity
- enough public evidence exists for a credible diagnosis
- legitimate public/permissioned contact route exists
- our production stack can materially help the identified problem
- recurring content need exists

REJECT/DEFER when:
- brand-new channel with no evidence
- prospect wants guaranteed growth
- requested work requires deceptive engagement, fake views, copyright abuse or impersonation
- no plausible economic reason to buy
- we cannot produce a useful channel-specific observation

## Discovery Questions
Use only after showing evidence.
1. Which outcome matters most over the next 60–90 days: reach, returning viewers, watch time, leads/sales, sponsorship readiness, or production consistency?
2. Which recent videos felt stronger or weaker than expected?
3. What is currently hardest: ideas, research, scripting, packaging, editing, consistency, or interpreting performance?
4. What part of the workflow do you want to keep personally?
5. If we build one Pilot, what would make you call it useful even before performance results arrive?

## Seller Conversation Framework — E.P.I.C.
**E — Evidence:** "I noticed X in your public channel data/content."  
**P — Problem hypothesis:** "That may indicate Y; I cannot see your private Studio data, so I would treat it as a hypothesis."  
**I — Intervention:** "I would test Z next. I prepared this small example so you can judge the thinking."  
**C — Commercial choice:** "If useful, the Pilot turns this into the complete production package. If not, keep the idea."

## Objection Handling
### “AI content is generic.”
Agree with the concern. The product is not access to AI; it is channel-specific research, editorial judgment, production and QA. Show the individualized evidence.

### “Can you guarantee views?”
No. Platform distribution and viewer behavior cannot be guaranteed. We guarantee the agreed work product and production process, not the audience outcome.

### “I can do this myself.”
Yes. The value proposition is saved production/research capacity and an external experiment, not secret access to tools. Offer the free scan and let usefulness decide.

### “Why should I trust you?”
Do not answer with adjectives. Show the channel-specific observation, explain what is fact vs hypothesis, show exactly what the Pilot contains, and use a small first transaction rather than asking for a long commitment.

### “Send pricing.”
First confirm scope. The Pilot should be one bounded experiment. Do not invent discounts or prices outside the approved commercial catalog.

## Prohibited Seller Behavior

- fake urgency/scarcity
- guaranteed growth/revenue
- pretending public data is private analytics
- mass unsolicited spam
- publishing/access without permission
- unapproved discounts or custom promises
- starting paid production before provider-verified order evidence

## Seller Training — 60 Minute Certification
### Module 1 — Product truth (10m)
Seller must explain Scan, Pilot, Momentum and Studio Ops without unsupported claims.

Pass test: distinguish deliverable guarantee from outcome guarantee.

### Module 2 — Qualification (10m)
Score three sample creators PASS/DEFER/REJECT and explain evidence.

Pass test: no sale attempt to an unqualified creator.

### Module 3 — Evidence selling (15m)
Prepare an EPIC conversation from a sample channel.

Pass test: facts/hypotheses/unknowns correctly labelled.

### Module 4 — Objections (10m)
Role-play guarantee, AI-generic, DIY and trust objections.

Pass test: no defensive claims or invented proof.

### Module 5 — Commercial gate (5m)
Seller must explain that checkout intent is not payment and production starts only after provider verification.

### Module 6 — Handoff (10m)
Complete the commercial-to-production brief with buyer objective, approved scope, proof artifact, promised deliverables, deadline, permissions and acceptance criteria.

Certification threshold: 90%; automatic fail for fabricated proof, guaranteed performance, unauthorized publishing or misrepresenting payment state.

## Manager Coaching Rule
Review one lost sale and one successful progression per seller per cycle. Diagnose whether failure was qualification, evidence quality, proof relevance, trust, scope, price, contact route or follow-through. Feed the lesson into the next Rescue Scan cohort.
